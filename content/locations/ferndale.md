You feel it in the quaint neighborhoods of craftsman-style homes, the colorful boutiques, and the street art that fills its walkable downtown. It's a warm, inviting, progressive city with a genuinely creative, bohemian spirit, and if you ever want a good time, one of Ferndale's summer festivals will show you exactly what I mean.
